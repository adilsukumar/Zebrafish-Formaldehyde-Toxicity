3. RELEVANCE TO AQUATIC TOXICITY
Erythrosine B is a heavily iodinated xanthene dye supplied commonly as its disodium salt. It is a solid, water-soluble colorant with substantial molecular weight. Because the molecule is ionic in its salt form, physicochemical descriptors such as logP must be interpreted carefully and should not be treated as simple neutral-organic values.

4. Working Interpretation
For Erythrosine B, the reviewed zebrafish literature clearly supports developmental toxicity, but I did not find a verified numerical Danio rerio LC50 in the accessible sources. The report therefore does not invent an LC50 value. The published study exposed embryos to 1–1000 µg/mL and reported concentration-dependent reductions in survival and other developmental endpoints through 96 hpf. If the practical requires a numerical LC50 specifically, this entry should be marked as 'not reported/verified' rather than substituting an LD50 or another fish species.
