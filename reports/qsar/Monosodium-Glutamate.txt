3. RELEVANCE TO AQUATIC TOXICITY
MSG is an ionic, highly water-soluble salt. In water it dissociates into sodium and glutamate ions, so volatilization is expected to be negligible. Its high water solubility and ionic character are important when interpreting aquatic exposure.

4. Working Interpretation
MSG produced a reported LC50 of 15,200 ppm at 48 hpf and 10,300 ppm at 96 hpf in zebrafish embryos. The study also reported developmental and cardiotoxic effects at lower concentrations. Because these are literature-derived values, they should be identified as literature data rather than QSAR Toolbox measurements.
