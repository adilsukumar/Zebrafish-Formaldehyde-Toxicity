3. RELEVANCE TO AQUATIC TOXICITY
Sunset Yellow FCF is a disulfonated azo dye and therefore exists predominantly as an ionic species in aqueous media. Its sulfonate groups promote water solubility and reduce the tendency for neutral-phase partitioning. These properties are relevant to aquatic exposure and zebrafish embryo toxicity.

4. Working Interpretation
The selected literature study reported an LC50 of 42.57 mM for Sunset Yellow FCF in developing zebrafish. The study also reported developmental abnormalities and mortality at higher concentrations. A separate recent study reported 416.67 ppm, demonstrating that LC50 values can vary with experimental design and units; the selected report retains the 42.57 mM value exactly as reported by the primary paper.
