3. RELEVANCE TO AQUATIC TOXICITY
Formaldehyde is a very small, highly reactive and highly volatile compound. Its low boiling point means that pure formaldehyde exists as a gas at ordinary temperature. It is highly soluble in water and is commonly handled as aqueous formaldehyde solutions. Its chemical reactivity, volatility and high water solubility are important when considering environmental exposure and aquatic toxicity.

4. Working Interpretation
Formaldehyde has a recorded Danio rerio mortality LC50 of 41 mg/L after 96 h. QSAR Toolbox profiling places it in Class 3 for acute aquatic toxicity and identifies aldehydes as the relevant aquatic toxicity mode of action. The ECOSAR classification is Aldehydes (Mono), while the Cramer profiler reports Low (Class I). The selected genotoxicity alert profilers report no alert found. Schiff base formation is identified by several mechanistic profilers.
